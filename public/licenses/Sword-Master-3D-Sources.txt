S’word Master 3D model sources

Monsters (12 models in public/models) were authored for this project using Blender.
Reproducible source: scripts/create_3d_assets.py

Hero (public/models/hero.glb) is converted from Kenney "Mini Characters" 2.0
(character-knight and weapon-sword), released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0).
Source files: assets/kenney/mini-characters
Conversion: scripts/import_kenney_hero.py
License text: Kenney-Mini-Characters-CC0.txt (www.kenney.nl)

Dungeon pieces (public/models/dungeon-kit.glb: floor, walls, columns, banners,
barrels, chest and other props) come from Kenney "Mini Dungeon" 2.0, CC0.
Source files: assets/kenney/mini-dungeon
Build script: scripts/build_kenney_dungeon.py
License text: Kenney-Mini-Dungeon-CC0.txt (www.kenney.nl)

Asset manifest: public/models/manifest.json
No model-generation service was used.

Three.js, React Three Fiber and their dependencies retain their own MIT licenses,
provided alongside this notice. Existing illustration and Pixelorama sources
remain separate from these 3D meshes.
